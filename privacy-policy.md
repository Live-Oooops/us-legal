# Us Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Us is a private shared space for two people. This Privacy Policy explains how the Us iOS application collects, uses, shares, and protects information.

## Information we collect

### Account information

Us uses Sign in with Apple as its only authentication method. We receive the Apple account identifier assigned to Us and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address or Apple private relay address. We do not receive your Apple ID password.

### Content you provide

We process the information you add to Us, including profile details, relationship dates, tasks, calendar events, wishes, savings plans, loyalty-card data, time capsules, important people, photos, documents, and related notes. Content in a connected couple space is shared with the other participant.

### Device and service information

We may process a push-notification token, basic device and app information, diagnostics, and security records needed to operate and protect the service.

### Product analytics

With your consent, Us sends limited product events to AppMetrica: first registration, the name of a viewed screen, creation of a couple, and addition of a task or wish. These events do not include the text, title, photo, file, barcode, note, invite code, or other private content you enter.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- authenticate you and maintain your account;
- connect two users into a shared couple space;
- synchronize and display shared content;
- store and deliver files you choose to upload;
- provide notifications you enable;
- maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures, and support account deletion;
- understand feature usage and improve Us when analytics consent is enabled; and
- comply with applicable legal obligations.

## Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We disclose information only as needed to provide Us, follow your instructions, protect users and the service, or comply with law.

- **Your partner.** Once a couple is connected, each participant can view and may be able to change or retain content in the shared space.
- **Apple.** Apple provides authentication, app distribution, and optional push-notification delivery.
- **Supabase.** Supabase provides authentication infrastructure, database, realtime synchronization, server functions, and file storage. The Us production project is hosted in the United States.
- **AppMetrica / Yandex.** AppMetrica processes technical product-analytics data when you consent. According to AppMetrica documentation, this data may be processed on Yandex servers in the European Union and the Russian Federation.
- **Authorities or safety recipients.** We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect the rights, safety, and integrity of users or the service.

## International transfers

Us and its service providers may process information outside your state or country. Where required, transfers are handled using safeguards provided by applicable law and the relevant service-provider terms.

## Retention

We retain account and shared-space data while it is needed to provide Us. When you delete your account, we delete your profile and personal account data. If a connected partner remains, shared content may remain available to that partner as their copy of the shared space. If no partner remains, the shared space and associated files are scheduled for deletion. Limited security, transaction, or backup records may remain temporarily where required for legal, fraud-prevention, or technical-recovery purposes.

## Your choices and rights

You can:

- hide your email address when using Sign in with Apple;
- decline analytics consent and still review the legal documents before signing in;
- manage notification permission in iOS Settings;
- sign out from Couple Settings;
- remove downloaded files from your device; and
- permanently delete your account from Couple Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of personal information, and to appeal certain decisions. Us does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising and does not use AppMetrica’s advertising-support module.

## Security

We use authenticated access, row-level database policies, encrypted network connections, private storage controls, and optional device authentication for shared files. No system can guarantee absolute security, so keep independent copies of important information and protect access to your Apple account and device.

## Children

Us is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us through the Us support page.

## Changes

We may update this policy to reflect product, legal, or service changes. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required.

## Contact

For privacy requests or questions, open a support request at https://github.com/Live-Oooops/us-legal/issues/new. GitHub issues are public, so do not include private content, invite codes, Apple credentials, or other sensitive information. To delete your account without contacting support, open **Us → Couple Settings → Delete account**.
